    How to read this

    • Spread. For each pair, the log price of the first stock minus a frozen multiple of the second. The multiple was fitted on 2018 to 2021 and never refitted.
    • Entry and exit. Open when the z-score reaches 2 in either direction, close when it comes back inside 0.5, or after 30 trading days.
    • Long and short spread. Long spread buys the first stock and sells the second. Short spread does the reverse.
    • Costs. 10 basis points per side on each leg, plus 50 basis points a year to borrow the shorted stock.
    • The pairs. No pair passed the strict multiple-testing screen, so the five lowest p-values were used and are labelled exploratory.
    • Test period. 2023 to 2024 was kept locked until the research choices were frozen.

    A replay of historical backtest results from the saved v1.0 run, with a one day execution lag. It is research, not a live trading system and not investment advice.
